YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Information we collect

Playroom does not ask for an account and does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information. The games work offline and do not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking.

Game scores and preferences

Game scores and records are saved only in local storage on your device so they are available when you return. They are not sent to us. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes them.

Children

Playroom is a general-audience collection of casual games. It does not knowingly collect information from children or anyone else.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version and date will be published here. For privacy questions, contact the app developer through the support contact shown on the Playroom Google Play listing.

Before publishing, host this policy at a public, permanent URL and add that URL to the Google Play Console.
